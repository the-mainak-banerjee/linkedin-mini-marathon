FAQ

Questions people ask us

Most of them are really one question: “Can someone like me actually do this?” Yes.

I don't know what to post about. Will this still help?
Yes. That's the most common place people start. Session 2 helps you pick one clear direction, and Session 5 walks through a post from blank page to finished post and image.
I'm an introvert. Do I have to be loud to grow?
No. A clear profile, thoughtful comments and well-written DMs work without being loud. Session 4 shows how comments alone can put you in front of new people.
I'm not a good writer.
You don't need to be. Clear, simple English works better on LinkedIn than fancy writing. Session 5 shows the exact process, step by step.
I'm busy. How much time does this take?
Six evenings across three weeks, all at 8 PM IST so they fit after work. Join only the sessions you need.
What if my colleagues or manager see my posts?
Then they see someone who knows their field and shares useful ideas. Building a personal brand isn't announcing that you're leaving. Done well, it makes you look good where you are.
Do I need a big following first?
No. Everyone starts at zero. The marathon is built for people starting small, and commenting is how you borrow reach before you have your own.
Can I join only some sessions?
Yes. Add only the sessions you want to your cart. Four are free. The two deep dives are ₹160 each until 5 December, and ₹250 after that.
Will this get me a job or clients?
We don't promise or guarantee any job, clients, followers or income. The marathon teaches you to build a personal brand on LinkedIn, and a strong brand makes it more likely that opportunities like clients, jobs and collaborations find you. What you do with it is up to you.
Will sessions be recorded?
Plan to join live. Recordings aren't confirmed yet, and the live parts, like the profile reviews in Session 1, only happen in the room.
